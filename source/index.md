# Gluu Product Documentation

This document helps you understand the current support status of a Gluu product version and provides links to the associated documents.

This schedule details the end-of-life dates for Gluu Server versions.

| Version | Status | Release Date | Community EOL Date | Enterprise EOL Date  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4.5.x | Active | February 2023 | December 2027 | December 2028 |
| 4.4.x | Active | May 2022 | January 2027 | June 2027 |
| 4.3.x | EOL | September 2021 | September 2024 | December 2025 |
| 4.2.x | EOL | July 2020 | December 2023 | December 2024 |
| 4.1.x | EOL | March 2020 | September 2022 | September 2023 |
| 4.0.x | EOL | October 2019 | April 2021 | April 2022 |
| 3.1.x | EOL | September 2017 | October 2020 | December 2021 |
| 3.0.x | EOL | June 2017 | December 2018 | December 2019 |
| 2.4.x | EOL | July 2016 | December 2018 | December 2019|

!!! note
    
    Versions that have reached end-of-life mark will not get security updates and other critical patches.If you want to continue getting the latest security updates, you’ll need a Gluu Subscription. 
    If you want free binary distributions, please check out the [Janssen Project](https://jans.io), a Gluu 4 fork contributed in 2020 to the Linux Foundation, and the home to a vibrant open source community.
